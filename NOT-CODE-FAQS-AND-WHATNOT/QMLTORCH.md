# QML Torch project brief

QML Torch is the shared CAIR Fall 2026 software project. It gives students a small, familiar PyTorch entry point for simulator-based quantum layers.

The team will maintain the layer API, beginner examples, tests, and one controlled comparison with a classical baseline. Results will be described with their limits. Quantum advantage is a question to measure, not a promise.

For the current plan, read [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](../docs/ROADMAP.md), [`docs/TEAM_SCOPE.md`](../docs/TEAM_SCOPE.md), and [`docs/MEETING_PLAN_FALL_2026.md`](../docs/MEETING_PLAN_FALL_2026.md).
