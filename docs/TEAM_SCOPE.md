# Team scope and ownership

QML Torch is a CAIR team repository. Contributions should improve the shared package, its tests, examples, or onboarding materials.

The repository does not contain a member's independent research notes, unpublished results, personal portfolio, or claims of authorship. Put those in the appropriate private or independently maintained project. A team contribution should have a clear issue, a small reviewable change, and a reproducible check.

The division lead sets the shared direction and reviews releases. Contributors own the changes they propose and receive credit in release notes or the relevant demo. The team may cite external work, but citations do not imply that the team reproduced or endorsed every claim.

## Current project boundary

In scope: the PyTorch-facing layer API, simulator examples, tests, beginner documentation, and one controlled classical baseline comparison.

Out of scope by default: dequantization research, paper replication, large datasets, hardware procurement, conference authorship promises, and independent research questions.
