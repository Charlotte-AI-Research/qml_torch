# CAIR QML application — Fall 2026

CAIR QML is accepting applications for the Fall 2026 team. Applications are due **August 30, 2026**; review may happen on a rolling basis, so early applications are welcome.

Everyone may attend weekly QML meetings. You can listen, ask questions, study the material, and learn at your own pace before taking on project responsibilities. Active contributors join a small project group and complete an agreed task each week.

No previous quantum-computing experience is required. We look for curiosity, reliability, basic programming experience, clear communication, and willingness to learn independently. Active contributors should plan for approximately **3–5 hours per week**.

## Application questions

1. Name
2. Student email
3. Academic year
4. LinkedIn or GitHub (optional)
5. Have you previously participated in CAIR QML? If yes, briefly describe your contribution; if no, write “N/A.” Attending meetings and learning counts as a valid answer.
6. If you are not selected as an active contributor, would you still like to attend general meetings?
7. How many hours can you realistically commit each week?
8. You are assigned an experiment using a library you have never used. What would you do first?
9. Why do you want to join QML?
10. How comfortable are you with Python? (1 = not yet comfortable, 5 = fluent)
11. Which tools or topics have you used? (Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, Git/GitHub, testing, data visualization, quantum computing, PennyLane, Qiskit, documentation, or other)
12. Would you be interested in presenting CAIR QML work at a conference or research event?
13. Please confirm: I can attend or communicate about my weekly commitment; I will ask for help early; I will keep shared work reproducible; I understand that quantum advantage is not promised; and I will keep independent work separate from this team repository.

## What happens next

We review preparation, interest, reasoning, availability, communication, and follow-through. Selected applicants may have a short conversation or a small onboarding task. Please monitor your UNC Charlotte email.
