This animation replays a recorded qml-torch training run. Two features are encoded as qubit rotations, three trainable rotations per qubit follow, CNOT gates entangle the qubits, Pauli-Z averages feed a small linear layer, and training lowers the loss from about 0.59 to 0.17. The model classifies all 16 held-out test examples correctly; a classical linear baseline does too. Keys: Space pauses, arrow keys change step, F toggles fullscreen.
